# Acro Party revision history

Versions before 1.0 are beta builds. **v1.0** will be the first finished release.
The version shows in the bottom-right corner of every screen.

| Version | Date | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v0.23 | Oct 7, 2026 | The lightning-round winner is always the champion and is ranked first in the final standings (even with fewer points); the other finalist is second, everyone else follows by points. Phones show the same placings. Versioning changed to beta numbers (v0.xx) ahead of v1.0. |
| v0.22 | Oct 7, 2026 | Lightning-round music remixed: brass pulled back about 14 dB; percussion brought forward with a 16th-note shaker, deep tom hits on every bar, rim clicks, tom fills and louder kick and claps. |
| v0.21 | Oct 7, 2026 | Added the Acro Party theme (your track): the full intro plays when a room opens, then its main section loops in the lobby; answer rounds loop a lighter section of the theme; the lightning round uses a 145 bpm remix made from the theme. Music fades out for the lightning results reveal. Synthesized music is kept for voting, results and the champion screen. |
| v0.20 | Oct 7, 2026 | Fixed: no way to unpause (the pause screen now has a large Resume game button, and the space bar pauses and resumes). Fixed: Ace's AI voice sometimes switched to the robot voice. Voice requests now queue and retry when ElevenLabs is busy, abandoned lines are skipped, and a failed line shows as a caption instead. |
| v0.19 | Oct 7, 2026 | The clock and entries wait for Ace: each round's reveal, the lightning intro and each lightning reveal hold until he finishes talking, then he says "Go!" as the clock starts (25-second safety limit). |
| v0.18 | Oct 7, 2026 | Lightning round visuals: hyperdrive stars (about 6x speed, rainbow colors), shockwave rings, a throbbing nebula and glowing letter tiles, pulsing with the music. |
| v0.17 | Oct 7, 2026 | Ace can use a lifelike ElevenLabs AI voice (default "Brian"; any voice ID, including your own), with the key kept on the server, line caching and a daily character cap. |
| v0.16 | Oct 7, 2026 | Deep announcer voice (male voices preferred, lower pitch); trippy space background with color swirls, nebula and a starfield that warps when letters appear. |
| v0.15 | Oct 6, 2026 | Answer timers grow with the letters (20 s for 3 letters, +5 s per extra letter) with optional kids' extra time (+10 or +20 s); game-show style music played by a synthesized band; Ace redrawn as a cartoon game-show host. |
| v0.14 | Oct 6, 2026 | Fixed: unable to type the room code, nickname or answers. Added the version label on every screen; browsers always load the latest files. |
| v0.13 | Oct 6, 2026 | Fixed: the screen stuck on Kid-friendly / Adult on networks that block live updates (automatic fallback to checking in once a second); error message if a room can't be created. |
| v0.12 | Oct 6, 2026 | Music, sound effects and a soft timer ticker on the TV; feedback sounds and last-5-seconds buzz on phones; sound settings and mute. |
| v0.11 | Oct 6, 2026 | Ace the announcer: welcomes players, calls letters, reads answers, announces winners, hosts the lightning round (browser voice or captions). |
| v0.10 | Oct 6, 2026 | First playable prototype: TV host screen and phone controllers, room codes and QR join, Kid-friendly and Adult modes (word filter, 18+ check), 5 to 8 rounds of 3 to 7 letters, anonymous voting, scoring, best-of-3 lightning round, host controls and automatic rejoin. |
