# Acro Party revision history

Versions before 1.0 are beta builds. **v1.0** will be the first finished release.
The version shows in the bottom-right corner of every screen.

| Version | Date | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v0.40 | Oct 26, 2026 | Lightning round: 15 s for the 3-letter acronym, 18 s for 4 letters and 21 s for 5 letters (20, 23 and 26 with kids' extra time); the TV, phones, Gene and How to Play say so. Phones stay awake during a game (screen wake lock, or a tiny silent looping video where that isn't available), reconnect instantly when unlocked or switched back to, and keep their seat and score for 10 minutes during a game. The server now supports byte ranges (needed for video on iPhones). |
| v0.39 | Oct 20, 2026 | The TV screen now scales with the display size (1rem = 16px at 1920x1080, proportionally smaller or larger elsewhere, up to 4K). Gene has his own strip along the bottom and shrinks on busy screens, so he never covers answers or scores. Automatic fit-to-screen shrinks the game area further if anything would still overflow. Results with many answers use two columns. Checked with a 10-player game at seven screen sizes from 800x600 to 3840x2160: every answer visible, nothing under Gene (the old layout failed 19 of 20 checks at 1280x720 and 960x540). |
| v0.38 | Oct 16, 2026 | New How to Play page (`/how-to-play`), opened from a 📖 How to play button on the game-mode screen. Step-by-step tutorial with screenshots from a real game: joining, random letters, voting, scoring (votes, most-votes bonus, speed bonus), the Lightning Round face-off (3, 4, 5 letters, 15 seconds each) with everyone else judging, and the champion. |
| v0.37 | Oct 9, 2026 | Smart TVs: new TV mode for smooth play on TV chips (no background blur, still backdrop, fewer stars at 30 fps, simpler lightning glow, recorded music only); turns on automatically in TV browsers and can be switched in the lobby. About 6x faster in a slowed-CPU test. New LG TV app (webOS .ipk) that opens the TV screen without the web browser and finds the game on the network automatically. Compatibility fixes for older TV browsers. |
| v0.36 | Oct 9, 2026 | Local network play: the join QR code is now made on the computer (works without internet; checked by decoding 60 test codes), the TV shows QR code and room code side by side with Wi-Fi tips and other addresses to try, Gene's lobby speech bubble no longer covers the QR code, and on Windows the game warns at start-up if the network is set to Public and explains the firewall setting. README has a home Wi-Fi section, including opening the TV screen on a smart TV or tablet. |
| v0.35 | Oct 9, 2026 | Scoring: if only one player answers a round, that answer wins outright and earns the full round-winner bonus (points equal to the number of letters) plus the speed point. Before, it only got the speed point. The TV and Gene announce it as the round winner. |
| v0.34 | Oct 9, 2026 | Lobby: removed the answer-timer note under the settings. Kids' extra time is now shown only in Kid-friendly games (Adult games don't offer it, and the server ignores it there). |
| v0.33 | Oct 9, 2026 | `node server.js` now also reads "Acro Party settings.txt" (before, only the desktop launcher did), so Gene's AI voice turns on either way. Also accepts "Acro Party settings.txt.txt" (Windows' hidden extensions) and files saved with a byte-order mark, and the start-up message says exactly where to put the file. |
| v0.32 | Oct 9, 2026 | Docs: how to play on Windows 11 with Smart App Control turned on (run the launcher with Node.js, which is signed, plus a desktop shortcut), and why an unsigned AcroParty.exe is blocked there. |
| v0.31 | Oct 9, 2026 | The Windows app builder now copies your private "Acro Party settings.txt" (ElevenLabs key and voice ID) from the windows folder next to AcroParty.exe. The file is never packed into the app and is excluded from GitHub. |
| v0.30 | Oct 8, 2026 | Windows app: the game can be built into a standalone AcroParty.exe (double-click to play, no Node.js needed). Includes a one-click builder, a settings file for the ElevenLabs key, an app window for the TV screen (Edge or Chrome), automatic fallback to the next free port, and smarter detection of the Wi-Fi address phones should use. |
| v0.29 | Oct 7, 2026 | Kid-friendly filter on phones: submitting an answer with a naughty word flashes a big "Naughty!" over the answer box (with a shake and a buzz), hides the word as ✱✱✱, then after about a second deletes just that word and puts the cursor there so the player can type a new one ("Oops! Try a different word for P."). Works in the main rounds and the lightning round. |
| v0.28 | Oct 7, 2026 | Both Kid-friendly welcome lines now carry the same idea: everyday letters and acronyms turn into something silly or completely different and score you points. |
| v0.27 | Oct 7, 2026 | New welcome line for Gene Woolery: "...this is Acro Party, the game show where your everyday acronyms turn into something completely different, and the funnier they get, the more points you score!" (replaces "bad ideas score points"). |
| v0.26 | Oct 7, 2026 | The host is now **Gene Woolery** (homage to Gene Rayburn and Chuck Woolery): new, more detailed artwork (an original cartoon 1970s host with feathered hair, burgundy tux, gold bow tie and a long skinny microphone) with softer ivory teeth instead of bright white. New mouth animation: six mouth shapes that follow the loudness of the AI voice (or a natural syllable rhythm with the browser voice and captions), plus blinking, nodding and raised eyebrows on big moments. Captions show his name. |
| v0.25 | Oct 7, 2026 | Fixed: Ace was cut off mid-sentence when the game moved on (most noticeable in the lightning round's 2nd entry when finalists answered quickly). He now always finishes his sentence; lines he hasn't started are dropped, and late timing lines like "Go!" and "Five seconds!" are skipped instead of spilling into the next moment. Break between rounds changed to 25 seconds. Final standings play the Acro Party theme from the top, matching the intro. |
| v0.24 | Oct 7, 2026 | Ace's speech bubble now appears at the exact moment his voice starts (no more caption ahead of the audio); clips are preloaded while the previous line plays. Lightning round has red urgency shading (red edges throbbing on the beat, red nebula, swirls and shockwaves, red-orange stars) and the letters shake harder. 35-second break after every voting round before the next round (or the lightning round) starts, with a "Next round in" countdown, leaving room for future ad placements. |
| v0.23 | Oct 7, 2026 | The lightning-round winner is always the champion and is ranked first in the final standings (even with fewer points); the other finalist is second, everyone else follows by points. Phones show the same placings. Versioning changed to beta numbers (v0.xx) ahead of v1.0. |
| v0.22 | Oct 7, 2026 | Lightning-round music remixed: brass pulled back about 14 dB; percussion brought forward with a 16th-note shaker, deep tom hits on every bar, rim clicks, tom fills and louder kick and claps. |
| v0.21 | Oct 7, 2026 | Added the Acro Party theme (your track): the full intro plays when a room opens, then its main section loops in the lobby; answer rounds loop a lighter section of the theme; the lightning round uses a 145 bpm remix made from the theme. Music fades out for the lightning results reveal. Synthesized music is kept for voting, results and the champion screen. |
| v0.20 | Oct 7, 2026 | Fixed: no way to unpause (the pause screen now has a large Resume game button, and the space bar pauses and resumes). Fixed: Ace's AI voice sometimes switched to the robot voice. Voice requests now queue and retry when ElevenLabs is busy, abandoned lines are skipped, and a failed line shows as a caption instead. |
| v0.19 | Oct 7, 2026 | The clock and entries wait for Ace: each round's reveal, the lightning intro and each lightning reveal hold until he finishes talking, then he says "Go!" as the clock starts (25-second safety limit). |
| v0.18 | Oct 7, 2026 | Lightning round visuals: hyperdrive stars (about 6x speed, rainbow colors), shockwave rings, a throbbing nebula and glowing letter tiles, pulsing with the music. |
| v0.17 | Oct 7, 2026 | Ace can use a lifelike ElevenLabs AI voice (default "Brian"; any voice ID, including your own), with the key kept on the server, line caching and a daily character cap. |
| v0.16 | Oct 7, 2026 | Deep announcer voice (male voices preferred, lower pitch); trippy space background with color swirls, nebula and a starfield that warps when letters appear. |
| v0.15 | Oct 6, 2026 | Answer timers grow with the letters (20 s for 3 letters, +5 s per extra letter) with optional kids' extra time (+10 or +20 s); game-show style music played by a synthesized band; Ace redrawn as a cartoon game-show host. |
| v0.14 | Oct 6, 2026 | Fixed: unable to type the room code, nickname or answers. Added the version label on every screen; browsers always load the latest files. |
| v0.13 | Oct 6, 2026 | Fixed: the screen stuck on Kid-friendly / Adult on networks that block live updates (automatic fallback to checking in once a second); error message if a room can't be created. |
| v0.12 | Oct 6, 2026 | Music, sound effects and a soft timer ticker on the TV; feedback sounds and last-5-seconds buzz on phones; sound settings and mute. |
| v0.11 | Oct 6, 2026 | Ace the announcer: welcomes players, calls letters, reads answers, announces winners, hosts the lightning round (browser voice or captions). |
| v0.10 | Oct 6, 2026 | First playable prototype: TV host screen and phone controllers, room codes and QR join, Kid-friendly and Adult modes (word filter, 18+ check), 5 to 8 rounds of 3 to 7 letters, anonymous voting, scoring, best-of-3 lightning round, host controls and automatic rejoin. |
