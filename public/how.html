How to play

Turn random letters into something hilarious

Every round you get a set of random letters. Make up your own phrase where each word starts with those letters, in order. Then everyone votes for their favorite. The funniest (and fastest) answers score the most points.

1

Join with your phone

The TV shows a QR code and a 4-letter room code. Scan the code (or type the address into your phone's browser), pick a nickname and an avatar, and you're in. No app to install.

When everyone's in, the host presses Start game and picks 5 to 8 rounds.

2

Get your random letters

Each round, Gene Woolery reveals a new set of random letters on the TV. Make up your own acronym: one word for each letter, in order, typed on your phone.

  • Rounds go 3, 4, 5, 6, then 7 letters (rounds 6 to 8 go back to 3, 4, 5).
  • You get 20 seconds for 3 letters, plus 5 seconds for each extra letter.
  • Your phone lights up each letter green as you go, so you know it fits.
  • You can change your answer until time runs out.
3

Everyone votes

When time's up, all the answers appear on the TV without names. Everyone has 30 seconds to vote for their favorite on their phone.

  • You can't vote for your own answer.
  • Vote every round: if you skip voting, the votes you received don't count.
4

Score points

+1for every vote your answer gets + lettersMost votes bonus: the round winner gets points equal to the number of letters (3 letters = +3) +1Speed bonus: the fastest answer that gets at least one vote +1if you voted for the round winner
Only one answer came in? It wins the round outright: most votes bonus plus the speed bonus.
⚡

The Lightning Round

After the last round, the top two players (the most points, earned mostly from votes) face off in the Lightning Round.

  • Three acronyms back to back: 3, then 4, then 5 letters.
  • Just 15 seconds for the 3-letter one, 18 for 4 letters and 21 for 5 letters (5 more each with kids' extra time). Think fast!
  • Answers stay hidden until both finalists have entered all three.
⚡

Everyone else is the judge

While the finalists race the clock, everyone else waits to vote. Then the judges get 30 seconds to pick A or B for each of the three pairs.

  • Most votes wins each pair. If the votes tie, the faster answer wins.
  • Win 2 of the 3 pairs to become the champion.
🏆

Crown the champion

The Lightning Round winner is the Acro Party champion, even if the other finalist had more points. Everyone else is ranked by points.

Then hit Play again for a rematch.
